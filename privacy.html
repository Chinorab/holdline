Privacy

No account

There is nothing to sign up for. Open the page and play.

No tracking

No analytics, no ads, no cookies, no profile of how you play. The game loads everything from this site and sends nothing back.

Progress stays on this device

Your journal, streak and settings are saved in this browser, on your headset. Clearing its site data erases them.

No camera access

Holdline never receives camera images. While you play it reads your hand positions and the surfaces in your room. It keeps only the spot where you placed the board, saved on your headset.
